Salesforce LWC – Hire, Train, Deploy (HTD) Model
"Sourced, trained, and deployed LWC engineers in 60 days."
"Zero cost to client for training, zero compromise on quality."

The Challenge

A global technology company required Salesforce Lightning Web Components (LWC) engineers within 2 months to meet rapid project expansion needs. The primary challenge was finding Java/.NET developers, training them in Salesforce LWC, and ensuring they were deployment-ready within a short timeframe.

Our Solution

We sourced Java/.NET engineers with strong programming backgrounds and conducted a 30-day Salesforce LWC training program using a client-curated curriculum. To ensure seamless integration, we deployed engineers for a 2-month trial period at no cost to the client before converting them to full-time temp employees.

Results Achieved

Successfully onboarded and trained Salesforce LWC engineers, ensuring fast project execution while minimizing client hiring costs by delivering deployment-ready professionals. Improved overall team performance and efficiency, consistently meeting strict project timelines.

Success Stories: "Excellence in Managed Services Scaling"

Salesforce Vlocity – Scalable Managed Teams Model
"Implemented HTD model for specialized Vlocity talent."
"Onboarded 10 engineers monthly with steady supply."

The Challenge

A global technology leader required specialized Salesforce Vlocity engineers to be hired and trained within a 6-month window. The challenge lay in finding highly specialized talent, aligning them with client-specific needs, and ensuring a steady onboarding pipeline.

Our Solution

We implemented a Hire-Train-Deploy model for Salesforce Vlocity engineers, providing a 30-day intensive training program based on a client-provided curriculum. We ensured client involvement in the final selection process and managed the steady onboarding of 10 engineers per month.

Results Achieved

Successfully delivered Salesforce Vlocity-trained engineers within strict deadlines, enabling cost-effective workforce scaling. Improved team retention and performance ensured the long-term success of the client's steady growth strategy.
