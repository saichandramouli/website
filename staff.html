Engineering Services – IoT, Embedded Systems & Hardware
"Engineered recruitment for speed, scale, and quality."
"150 hires. 3 months. Zero compromise on quality."

Client Challenge

A multinational engineering services company required the rapid hiring of 200 skilled professionals in IoT, Embedded Systems, and Hardware Engineering within a tight 3-month timeline. Competing with leading global brands for niche talent made the recruitment process highly challenging.

Our Solution

We implemented a tailored RPO model to streamline hiring, led by a senior specialist with 10+ years of experience and supported by a dedicated sourcing team. By leveraging multiple channels like referrals, headhunting, social media, and internal databases, we ensured access to quality talent while optimizing the process through digital screening and targeted recruitment strategies.

Results Achieved

We successfully hired and onboarded 1,000 skilled professionals within six months, enabling the client to establish a strong operational presence in India and scale efficiently. The streamlined hiring approach improved talent quality while reducing overall recruitment complexity.
