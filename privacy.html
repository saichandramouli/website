Corporate Compliance

Privacy Policy

People Prime Worldwide respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you interact with our staffing services in accordance with Indian laws such as Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of personal information to offer you staffing services:

Personal Information: Name, contact details (email, phone number), date of birth, gender, address.

Professional Information: Resume, work experience, education, certifications, and job preferences.

Sensitive Personal Data or Information (SPDI): Health status, background check results, or any other information required by law or for employment purposes.

Usage Data: IP address, browser type, and other technical data when you use our website or services.

Any other information you voluntarily provide in recruitment process through interviews or assessment.

Information from interviews and phone-screenings you may have, if any.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used for the following purposes:

To match you with relevant job opportunities.

To communicate about job applications, interviews, and job offers.

To comply with legal requirements, including background checks and verification.

To improve our services and your experience.

Consent to Collect and Use Your Information

By sharing your information with us, you give your consent for us to collect, store, and use it for the purposes outlined above and we will ask for your consent before performing any background checks or gathering sensitive information, if applicable.

Data Retention

We will retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. Once your data is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymize it.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

Clients (Employers): To match you with job opportunities, we may share your resume and contact details with prospective employers.

Third-party Service Providers: We may share your data with third-party vendors who assist us with services like background checks, technical support, and data storage. These providers are required to protect your data and use it only for the intended purpose.

Legal Compliance: If required by law, we may disclose your data to government authorities, law enforcement agencies, or other legal entities in accordance with Indian law.

Your Rights

Under Indian law, you have the right to:

Correction: can request corrections to any inaccurate or incomplete information.

Deletion: You can request the deletion of your personal data, subject to legal or contractual obligations.

Withdrawal of Consent: You can withdraw your consent at any time. This will not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details provided below.

Security of Your Information

We use reasonable security practices and procedures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Children’s Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that we have collected personal data from a child under 18, we will take steps to delete that information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy and Consent Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. Any changes will be communicated through the updated policy with the revised effective date.
