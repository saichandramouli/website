Insurance
IBM and Microsoft announced Imagine Enterprise on 10 Nov, an agentic platform for building, running and governing
AI agents, launching with industry frameworks for financial services, insurance and retail. IBM Consulting contributes
more than 6,000 pre-built agents; Microsoft provides the AI, cloud and security technology.
IBM Newsroom

platforms, clinical guidelines and CMS datasets, with launch customers including HCA Healthcare, Northwell Health
and Optum. Separately, research among 182 operators reports 52.7% already running a live agentic workflow; it is
published by a firm that sells into the market it surveyed.

Manufacturing & industrials
Agile Robots, a Munich physical-AI company whose robots automate industrial work, raised a $500M Series F on 12
Nov led by SoftBank, with NVIDIA, Hitachi and T.Capital named as participants. The company says its German plant
can build up to 10,000 legged and wheeled humanoid robots a year.
Agile Robots

Automotive & mobility
UNQUALIFIED
Executives at Nexperia and STMicroelectronics said on 10 Nov that chip capacity moving to AI data
centres and robotics is tightening supply of the mature-node components used in vehicles and industrial equipment,
pointing manufacturers towards longer procurement horizons. This is an AI-driven supply constraint, not an adoption
item.
Mfg briefing

Retail & e-commerce
OpenAI's wallet asks retailers not to block agents and to make risk systems recognise them, while one major travel
merchant went the other way. The IBM–Microsoft platform also covers retail.
OpenAI · IBM

Consumer goods
UNQUALIFIED
The president of Lowes Foods told a grocery conference, reported 10 Nov, that AI use among
shoppers is already widespread, citing a projection that 80% of shoppers will interact with AI by 2030 and putting the
2026 figure at 74%. Both are projections he cited, not his company’s data. Coty was separately reported piloting AI
discovery across six brands.
