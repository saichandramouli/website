CEO TRANSITION · 12 NOV 2026 · UNITED STATES
Nvidia's chief executive resigned and the CFO took the role on an interim basis —
the board's filing addresses the part buyers care about
In a filing dated 12 November, Nvidia said Jensen Huang had resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer and a board member, effective immediately.
Colette Kress, Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, will serve as Interim Chief Executive Officer and continue as CFO until a permanent
successor is named. The board is searching for one and will consider internal and external candidates. Harvey C. Jones was appointed the company's
first-ever Chair of the Board. Kress has been CFO since September 2013, after nearly 25 years in finance roles at technology companies including Cisco
and Microsoft.
The filing gives no reason. It followed widely reported allegations about the former chief executive's personal conduct, which the filing does not address
and this brief does not report on. What the filing does address is continuity: the chair's statement speaks to operations, customer commitments and the
product roadmap, quoted below.
Open: NVIDIA Form 8-K, 12 Nov 2026 · CNN
OUR SUMMARY · WHAT A BUYER SHOULD TAKE FROM IT
A board that names continuity of customer commitments in its first statement is
telling you where it expects the questions to come from. For buyers the exposure
is not the person; it is concentration. If your AI roadmap assumes one supplier's
parts, prices and cadence, a leadership change there is a change to your plan.
