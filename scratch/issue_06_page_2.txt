COMPANY RESULTS · 28–30 OCT 2026 · SOUTH KOREA, NORTH AMERICA, EUROPE
A memory maker, a processor maker and an IT-services firm report growth; SK hynix says
supply is not keeping pace and Intel's CEO prioritises more supply
COMPANY · DATE
WHAT IT REPORTED
DEMAND AND SUPPLY, IN THE COMPANY'S WORDS
SOURCE
SK hynix
28 Oct · memory chips
3Q26 revenue 104.6 trillion won, up 328% year on
year; operating margin 80%. Preliminary and
unaudited.
Supply “is not keeping pace with the growth of the
market”; expects “the structure in which demand exceeds
supply to persist through 2030” (company outlook, not an
independent forecast).
R1
Intel
29 Oct · processors
3Q26 revenue $16.5B, up 21%; Data Center and AI
segment $7.3B, up 78%. Q4 guidance $15.0–16.0B
(company forecast).
Growth led by the Data Center and AI segment (our
reading: the segment added about $3.2B, more than the
group's total increase). CEO Lip-Bu Tan: “Our immediate
priority is to unlock additional supply to meet extraordinary
demand for our Xeon family.” (per company release). The
release does not attribute the growth to AI.
R24
Capgemini
30 Oct · IT services
Q3 revenue €6,073M, up 11.7% at constant
currency; bookings €5,401M, up 3.4%. 2026 growth
outlook raised to +9.0% to +9.5% (constant
currency; company forecast). Growth includes
acquisitions (notably WNS and Cloud4C), which the
company says had a “material contribution to
growth” in North America, UK & Ireland and Asia-
Pacific; it estimates about 5 points of 2026 growth
is inorganic and gives no Q3 organic figure.
CEO Aiman Ezzat: “Clients are now focused on deploying
AI at enterprise scale” (per company release). The release
cites clients “transforming their business operations with
agentic AI”.
