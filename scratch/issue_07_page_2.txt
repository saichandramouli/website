CFO SURVEY AND COMPANY RESULTS · 2–4 NOV 2026 · NORTH AMERICA, GLOBAL
Buyers of AI are still waiting for the return; two technology suppliers posted fast
growth in the same week — and the survey points at execution capacity
RGP surveyed 400 CFOs and equivalent finance leaders at organisations with annual revenue of $250M or more. 52% report positive AI returns today,
but just 10% describe them as significant; 54% expect significant positive returns within 24 months. 89% are positive about their organisation's
financial outlook for the next 12 months, while 49% say their transformation agenda exceeds their organisation's ability to execute. The split is worth a
board discussion: among CFOs whose agenda exceeds capacity, 24% report positive AI returns, against 79% among those whose capacity meets or
exceeds demand. Both halves of that comparison are the same respondent's own assessment, so it shows an association, not a cause, and the survey
did not ask about technology. Survey conducted September 2026; respondents in North America, Europe and Asia Pacific, per CFO.com. RGP sells transformation and
execution services — the capability its survey finds respondents lack. Open: CFO.com report · RGP survey release, syndicated copy
