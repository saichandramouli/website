 Put a date on the return. For each AI programme, name the quarter a
measured result lands and who signs for it.
 Test capacity, not just the case. Rank AI initiatives against delivery
capacity before approving the next one.
 Price the architecture. Ask for tokens and latency per task, not just
accuracy, before approving multi-agent designs.
 Start simple. Require a single-agent baseline before any team builds a
coordinated one.
 Inventory agent frameworks. List the open-source agent software in your
stack and in your vendors', with version and owner.
 US national banks: align to the examiner. Map AI uses in credit,
payments, fraud and operations onto your existing risk framework.
 Fix measurement before scaling. If you cannot show a return on today's
agents, do not add more.
 Look inside first. Before hiring for an AI role, ask which current
employees could be redeployed into it.
