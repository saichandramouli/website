L E A D E R S H I P AC T I O N S T H I S W E E K
↑ Agenda
1. Decide your agent policy explicitly. Name the executive who owns
whether AI agents may browse, buy and manage accounts on your
channels.
2. Find out what your site does today. Your firewall and bot rules already
answer this question, with no policy behind them.
3. Write down who you refund when an agent buys on a customer's behalf
and the order is disputed.
4. Inventory your partner and supplier portals against the FBI's list of entry
points, not just your main estate.
5. Check every AI line item against current list prices, including any that are
introductory and expire this month.
6. Ask whether your AI roadmap depends on one supplier's leadership,
and what your contract says about continuity.
7. If you operate in the EU, name who would own an AI Act sandbox
application ahead of the regulation's 1 December date of effect.
8. Ask HR for hours, age and education trends in your most exposed roles,
not just headcount.
