L E A D E R S H I P A C T I O N S T H I S W E E K
↑ Agenda
1. Measure value, not activity. Ask each unit for one AI use case
with a revenue, cost or cycle-time baseline. ›
2. Lock in capacity. Check that cloud and hardware contracts
cover the AI capacity your plans assume. ›
3. Re-run rejected cases. Re-price AI business cases turned down
on cost at this week's lower token prices (per OpenAI). ›
4. Tighten vendor terms. Add data separation, incident notice and
audit rights to AI vendor contracts. ›
5. Gate agent actions. Require a check before AI agents move
money, data or customer records. ›
6. Confirm patching. Ask IT whether self-hosted AI serving
software (vLLM, SGLang) is on the fixed versions. ›
7. Set a skills target. Publish an AI-skills goal with progress
reporting, as SAP India does. ›
8. India operations: review MeitY's DEPA draft on AI training with
partner data; it invites recommendations. ›
