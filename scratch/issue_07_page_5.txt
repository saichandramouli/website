New research published by Careerminds reports that 92% of US HR
professionals and 88% of US people managers believe employees within their
organisations could be reskilled for AI-related roles, yet organisations are
more likely to hire externally than redeploy workers already on the payroll. The

Hiring over Reskilling. Survey of 595 US HR professionals and 547 US people
managers, aged 2565, at organisations with 500 to 10,000 or more
employees, conducted 1526 May 2026 with Censuswide.
