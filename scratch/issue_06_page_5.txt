INDUSTRIAL · JAPAN · 28 OCT 2026 · ANNOUNCED PLAN
Hitachi to launch a 35,000-person digital services company with physical AI as a named
focus
Hitachi plans to launch Hitachi Digital Inc. on 1 April 2027, uniting GlobalLogic and Hitachi Digital Services: about 35,000 professionals
in 27 countries serving more than 880 active clients. Named focus areas are physical AI, data and intelligence, cloud and site
reliability engineering, and enterprise applications. An announced plan; the company is not yet operating.
Source: Hitachi announcementR7
BUSINESS LENS · FROM THE SOURCE

people serving more than 880 clients.

INDIA · MUMBAI · 28 OCT 2026 · COMPANY PROGRAMME
SAP India adds programmes toward 2 million people
with AI skills by 2030
SAP announced more programmes toward equipping two million people in
India with AI-ready skills by 2030, part of a global pledge to help 12 million.
SAP India says nearly 13% of the two million have already taken part through
certification, student education and community programmes. Target and
progress figures are the company's own.
